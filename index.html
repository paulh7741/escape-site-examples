Escape Hairdressing, refreshed

A review of the current site and three homepage directions. The site's job is simple: get people booked in. The price list, opening hours and Setmore booking page already exist. Most of the problems come from where they sit on the site.

You're seeing prices you changed in the demo editor (saved only in this browser).

What's already good

Real prices and opening hours are on the site, and there's an online booking page on Setmore. The salon has 25 years of history, a 2018 customer service award, and warm reviews that mention stylists by name.

What's holding it back

The booking button is hard to find, the phone link is malformed, and the cookie banner doesn't meet current UK rules. The © 2023 footer and old photos also make the site look unattended.

No rebuild needed

The site runs on WordPress (up to date, 7.1) with the Enfold theme. Every fix below can be done in the existing admin. Each prototype can be built in Enfold's page builder or a newer block theme.

Updating prices: one list, edited once

Right now the prices sit in a hand-built table on one page. Any "from £30" wording elsewhere has to be changed separately. In the new design, the salon keeps one price list and every page reads from it: the homepage cards, the full menu and the booking picker. The "from" prices on the homepage are worked out automatically.

Recommended

Keep WordPress, add one "Prices" screen

Whoever builds the new theme adds a single Prices page to the WordPress admin, which looks like the demo editor linked below. She logs in as she does now, changes a number and clicks Save. No page builder and no tables.

No developer: TablePress plugin

A free plugin that stores the price list as a spreadsheet-style table and shows it on any page with a shortcode. It's less polished, but she could set it up herself this week.

Also: raise everything at once

When prices go up across the board, one "raise all prices by £2 or 5%" button saves editing 17 cells one by one. The demo editor includes it.

What to fix, most important first

Each item says what's wrong and what to change.

High

The online booking link is hidden

The homepage tells people to "use the booking link", but the homepage has no booking link. The only link to Setmore is halfway down Services & Prices. The "Book with us" buttons go to the Contact page instead.

Fix: put a "Book online" button in the header on every page, linking to escapehairdressing.setmore.com. On phones, add a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen with "Book online" and "Call".

High

The header phone link is malformed

The "Phone to book" link in the header dials +4401316526050, which has an extra 0 after the country code. Some phones and apps won't connect that number.

Fix: link to tel:+441316526050 and write the number as 0131 652 6050 so it's easy to read.

High

The cookie banner doesn't meet UK rules, and one analytics tag is dead

The banner says "by continuing to browse… you agree". Under UK GDPR and PECR, that doesn't count as consent. Google Analytics also loads before the visitor makes any choice. On top of that, the site still loads an old Universal Analytics tag (UA-124574049-1), and Google shut that service down in 2023.

Fix: remove the UA tag. Then either install a proper consent plugin (such as Complianz or CookieYes) or drop analytics cookies altogether. A smaller banner will also stop covering a third of the phone screen.

Medium

People can't tell who's who, or what "Director" means

Prices come in three tiers: Designer, Director and Senior Director. Nothing explains them or says which stylist is which. The About page is titled "Meet the team" in Google, but there's no team on it. The reviews mention Louise, Jenna and Rachel by name.

Fix: add a short team section with a photo, name, level and specialism for each stylist. Add one line explaining the tiers, and link each person to their Setmore booking.

Medium

It looks unattended

The footer says © 2023. The gallery photos date from 2018–2021, and the headings are all capitals in pale grey on white, which is hard to read. The Instagram account is probably more current than the website.

Fix: make the footer year update automatically, darken the body text, and replace the gallery slider with a grid of 6–9 recent photos from Instagram.

Medium

Colour clients hit a dead end

Colour appointments are phone-only and the site doesn't say why. The salon is open 9.30am–3pm on Tuesdays, so evening browsers can't do anything until the next day.

Fix: explain it briefly ("colour needs a patch test 48 hours before, so we like to chat first"). Then either add a free "colour consultation" appointment in Setmore or a "request a callback" form.

Medium

Mixed messages about men's cuts

The description Google shows says "hairstyles and colours for men and women", and the Services page mentions options for men. The homepage says "for Women" only, and the price list has no men's prices.

Fix: decide whether to offer men's cuts. Then either add men's prices or remove "men" from the Google description and the Services page.

Low

Reviews are anonymous and undated

There are three quotes in a slider, labelled only "Google Review" or "Facebook Review".

Fix: show the Google star rating and review count, with first names and a month, and link to the full Google reviews.

Low

Small text errors

"newletter" (newsletter), "you can chose" (choose), plus typos inside the quoted reviews ("Being going", "walk our feeling"). It's fine to tidy obvious slips in the quotes.

Low

Loose ends

TikTok appears only on the Contact page. Every page has a newsletter sign-up, which is only worth keeping if someone actually sends a newsletter. The opening hours in the footer are misaligned. An SEO plugin like Yoast or Rank Math can add the salon's address and hours for Google ("LocalBusiness" data). Also check that the Google Business Profile matches the website.

Quick wins for one afternoon

These can all be done in the WordPress admin, with no redesign. Ticks are saved in your browser.

The three directions

All three keep the real prices, hours and reviews, and all three put booking in the header and in a bar fixed to the bottom on phones. They differ in look and in how much work they take. Each prototype can switch between desktop and phone view.
