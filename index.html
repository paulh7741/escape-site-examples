Good to know

Anything else? Call us on 0131 652 6050 and we'll happily help.

Why do I need to call to book colour?

Colour needs a quick patch test at least 48 hours before your appointment. A short chat also means we book the right amount of time for the look you want.

What do Designer, Director and Senior Director mean?

They're our stylists' experience levels, and prices vary a little between them. Every level does great work. Pick the one that suits your budget.

Do you offer student discount?

Yes. Students get 10% off on Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays. Just bring your student card.

Can I bring my children?

Of course. Children's cuts start at £10 for under-5s.
