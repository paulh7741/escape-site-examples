What clients say

Questions

Why is colour booked by phone?

Colour needs a patch test at least 48 hours before. A quick call means we book the right time for the look you want.

What are the stylist levels?

Designer, Director and Senior Director reflect experience. Prices vary a little, and every level does great work.

Do I need an account to book?

No. Choose a time on our booking page, add your name and number, and you're done.

Can I bring my children?

Of course. Children's cuts start at £10 for under-5s, and £16 for ages 5 to 10.
